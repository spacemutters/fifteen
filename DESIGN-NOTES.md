# Fifteen — design notes

**What it is:** paste a business's website, get a 15-second motion ad built from that site's own words, colors and logo. Runs in the browser and downloads as a video file. Free, with no sign-up and no payment.
**Who it's for:** owners of small local businesses, and the people who build sites for them (Davis's spec-site outreach).
**The one action:** paste a website address and press "Make the ad".

## Palette: a self-healing cutting mat

Fifteen is a cutting tool: you hand it raw footage (a website) and it trims that to fifteen seconds. The object that feels like that is the green cutting mat on an editor's desk: deep bottle green, a pale grid on top, a scalpel line through it. The accent is the mat's green. The paper is the pale gray-green of its printed grid. The ink is a near-black that keeps a little of the green.

| Token | Light | Dark | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| paper | `#EEF1EC` | `#0E1612` | ground |
| ink | `#121714` | `#E6ECE7` | body text — 15.91 / 15.34 : 1 |
| muted | `#4F5A53` | `#9AA79F` | captions — 6.31 / 7.36 : 1 |
| accent | `#1F5A42` | `#7DBE9C` | the one button, links — 7.09 / 8.51 : 1 on paper |
| line | ink @ 10% | ink @ 10% | hairlines |

Button text is the paper color on the accent: 7.09 : 1 in light mode, 8.51 : 1 in dark. The site follows the system light/dark setting, with no toggle.

This avoids the three default looks: no warm cream with a terracotta serif, no near-black with acid green, no zero-radius hairline grid.

## Type

- Display: **Archivo**, with the width axis. Tight and condensed, it reads like the text on an edit timeline.
- Body: **Instrument Sans**.
- Utility: **JetBrains Mono**, only for timecode (00:15) and the domain label inside the ad.
- The ad's own looks also load **Fraunces** for "Editorial". It's used inside the video only, never on the page.

Scale: display clamp(2.75rem, 1rem + 6.4vw, 7.5rem) · h1 clamp(2rem, 4.2vw, 3.5rem) · h2 1.5–2rem · lead 1.125–1.5rem · body 17px · label 12px uppercase +0.08em.

## Shape

One radius, 10px, on everything. Pills only for the timecode chip. Borders over shadows.

## Signature moment

The hero is a working player. A real 9:16 ad, generated by the same engine from Fifteen's own words, plays on loop beside the address box, with a running timecode under it. When you paste a site and press the button, that same frame becomes your ad. Nothing else on the page moves except quiet one-time reveals.
